Frequently Asked Questions

Does DocumentIQ give legal advice?

No. DocumentIQ provides automated informational risk detection to accelerate review. It is an intelligent assistive tool and does not replace qualified attorney counsel.

How does it prevent false risk alarms?

The 2.0 Engine features bidirectional negation understanding. For example, clauses explicitly stating "no termination penalty applies" are correctly classified as protective rather than penalizing.

Can I compare opposing redlines?

Yes! The Version Compare module performs side-by-side clause alignment, revealing added liabilities, removed guarantees, and subtle wording modifications.

Can I export PDF reports for clients or management?

Yes. With one click, generate comprehensive executive audit PDFs complete with severity summaries, extracted obligations, and redline recommendations.
