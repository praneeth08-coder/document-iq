Confidentiality is fundamental to contract review. DocumentIQ is architected with strict data isolation:

  • In-Memory Processing: Uploaded contract binaries are analyzed entirely in memory and never permanently written to disk.
  • No External Model Training: Your legal text is never transmitted to commercial AI providers or used to train public machine learning datasets.
  • Session & User Isolation: SQLite data stores are strictly partitioned by authenticated user ID with encrypted sessions.
Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Common Questions

Does DocumentIQ provide official legal advice?

No. DocumentIQ provides automated informational indicators based on predefined legal rules and pattern recognition. It assists your review process but does not substitute for qualified attorney counsel.

Can I compare two different draft agreements?

Yes. The Version Compare tool matches clauses between an original agreement and a proposed redline to highlight additions, removals, and subtle phrase modifications.

Are scanned image PDFs supported?

DocumentIQ analyzes text-based PDFs and documents. Scanned image-only PDFs without an embedded OCR text layer will prompt you to provide a searchable PDF.

What happens when I delete an analysis?

When you delete an analysis from your History, all stored clause findings, summaries, and metrics are permanently purged from your account database.
