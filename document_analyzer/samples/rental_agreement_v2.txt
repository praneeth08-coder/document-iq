RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

1. Term
This agreement starts on 1 January 2027 and runs for twelve months.

2. Rent and Payment
The tenant shall pay rent of Rs. 27,500 per month, due on or before the 5th day of each month.
A late fee of 5% of the monthly rent will be charged if rent is paid after the due date.
The tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 50,000 before moving in.
The tenant shall pay maintenance charges of Rs. 1,500 per month.

3. Renewal
This agreement renews automatically for successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives ninety (90) days' written notice before the end of the term.
The landlord may increase the rent on renewal.

4. Cancellation
The tenant may terminate this agreement early by giving 30 days written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two months' rent.
The security deposit is refundable within 30 days of the tenant leaving, less any agreed deductions.

5. Liability
The landlord shall not be liable for any loss or damage to the tenant's belongings.
The tenant agrees to indemnify the landlord against all claims arising from the tenant's use of the property.

6. Privacy
The landlord may collect personal information about the tenant for verification. The landlord does not sell your personal information to third parties.

7. Changes
The landlord reserves the right to modify these house rules at any time without notice.
